Reminders

Your three monthly reminders

Reminder 1 — Day before rent is due (last day of each month): Rent is due tomorrow. Prepare for your incoming monthly money.
Reminder 2 — Payday and rent payment (1st of each month): Your monthly income has arrived. Reserve the rent amount right away and submit your SHA rent payment.
Reminder 3 — Payment verification (2nd of each month): Did you pay your rent? Check that SHA received and posted it, and save your confirmation.
